=== STAGE 1.5 — DEEP-RESEARCH VERIFICATION (multi-engine) ===

Purpose: an independent, wide-net factual verification pass run in PARALLEL across
external deep-research engines (e.g. Gemini, GPT, Copilot) AFTER the Stage 1 research
brief is written and BEFORE the cross-LLM review stages (Stage 2 Bias Audit, Stage 3
Fact Verification). Stage 1.5 is NOT a framing or bias pass (that is Stage 2) and NOT a
single-engine fact-check (that is Stage 3). It is a redundancy layer: catch factual
errors and surface missing PRIMARY sources while the facts are still soft — before they
harden into cards. Verification responsibility is shared across all stages (see
CLAUDE.md Accuracy Standard); Stage 1.5 widens that net at the cheapest point to fix.

This preamble is read by the human operator who runs the external engines, and by the
Claude session that reconciles their returns. It does not block the build.

--- WHAT TO HAND THE ENGINES ---

Give each engine the same scoped task:
  1. The brief's "STAGE 1.5 — DEEP-RESEARCH VERIFICATION TARGETS" list (the prioritised
     claims that most need triangulation). If the brief has no such list, hand over every
     number, date, named actor, law/section, court case, and direct quote in the brief.
  2. This instruction: "For each item, find the PRIMARY source (official document,
     gazette, regulator publication, court/tribunal ruling, statute text, peer-reviewed
     paper, or a named authoritative outlet that cites and links such a source). Return
     the source, the exact figure/wording it supports, its date, and a direct link.
     If you cannot find a primary source, say so explicitly. Do not fill gaps from
     memory. Flag any claim where reputable sources disagree, and give both."

Run the engines independently. Do not let one engine see another's answer — the value is
in disagreement surfacing.

--- HOW CLAUDE RECONCILES THE RETURNS ---

For every claim in the brief, after reading all engine returns, tag it with exactly one:

  CONFIRMED (1.5)   — at least one engine traced it to a primary (or
                      primary-citing) source that matches the brief. Record the source.
  CORRECTED (1.5)   — an engine found the brief's figure/date/wording is wrong.
                      Replace it in the brief, note the old → new, cite the source.
  CONTESTED (1.5)   — engines or sources disagree, or none reached a primary source.
                      Keep the claim flagged; either soften it, attribute it, drop it,
                      or hand it to Stage 3 as a priority chase. Never publish a
                      CONTESTED specific as a bare fact.

Rules:
  - A claim only graduates to a card on CONFIRMED, or on CORRECTED with its corrected
    value re-confirmed. CONTESTED specifics must be softened, attributed, or dropped.
  - Engine output is itself a SECONDARY source until you open the primary it points to.
    An engine "confirming" a number is a lead, not a verification — follow the link.
  - The four cardinal sins still govern (overclaim, underclaim, misleading framing,
    unverified detail). Stage 1.5's job is to kill the fourth before it spreads.
  - Where the brief already flags a known accuracy trap (e.g. two claims that must not
    be collapsed), Stage 1.5 must explicitly resolve which side of the line each source
    supports — that trap is the highest-priority target.
  - 3R claims (Race, Religion, Royalty) carry the higher bar: require TWO independent
    primary sources or drop, regardless of how many engines "confirm" from secondary
    material.

--- OUTPUT ---

Append a "## STAGE 1.5 RECONCILIATION" section to the brief listing each target claim,
its tag, the primary source found (or "no primary found"), and any correction applied.
This becomes the audit trail Phase 6 (Legal + Accuracy Check) walks against the final
text. Carry CONTESTED items forward as the priority queue for Stage 3.
