## Why it slips under everyone's noses (the meta-answer)

1. **Attribution-by-design (cut-outs).** The IGP stated recruits "were only given certain tasks and did not know the true identities of their employers" (Malay Mail, 17 Nov 2023). The visible crime is real and the people caught are *telling the truth* that they never met a foreign handler. Case closes as ordinary crime. **[VERIFIED]**
2. **The mobster/spy ambiguity is itself cover.** Avitan reads three ways — organized-crime vendetta (Israeli press), Mossad op (Malaysian police), gun smuggler (the charge). He was convicted only on firearms. When an op reads three ways, none stick. **[VERIFIED]**
3. **Conviction ≠ exposure.** Firearms/immigration charges are easy to prove; espionage needs a confession or intercept. The system lands the provable lesser charge and buries the bigger question — 7 years for Avitan (firearms), 10 years + 6 strokes for his supplier Abdul Azim Yasin (consorting), zero espionage charges across all cases. **[VERIFIED]**
4. **A border that can be bought.** ~50 immigration officers were arrested/transferred by MACC in Sept 2024 for "counter-setting" (waving undocumented foreigners through); further busts ran through 2025. "Phantom travel" syndicates erase entry/exit records entirely. A clean passport plus a bought counter defeats screening before tradecraft is even needed. **[VERIFIED]**
5. **Low counter-intelligence culture.** The apparatus is built for terrorism/subversion, not state-on-state HUMINT, so espionage is rarely the first hypothesis and the proving data is never gathered. **[PLAUSIBLE]**
