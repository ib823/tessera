## ISSUE

The Public Accounts Committee's August 2022 report found the government had paid Boustead Naval Shipyard (BNS) RM6.083 billion for the Littoral Combat Ship (LCS) programme without a single ship completed, with RM1.4 billion used for other purposes including cost overruns. The contract was restructured in May 2023 to five ships at about RM11.2 billion, and the shipyard passed to MOF Inc ownership as Lumut Naval Shipyard (LUNAS). At this issue's source date (17 May 2026) the first ship was in sea trials and no ship had been delivered; by then MINDEF had reported about RM8.3 billion spent, not RM6.08 billion.

Sister issue: 1805 covers the same programme (brief `lcs-cost-now-rm112-billion-first-ship-in-sea-trial`). The two issues conflict on the signing year and on spending; see CONTRADICTIONS.

## PERIOD

December 2011 (letter of award) to October 2026.

## CONTEXT (timeline with sources)

- 16 Dec 2011: letter of award to BNS; final contract signed 17 Jul 2014 at RM9 billion, per the forensic audit as reported by The Sun [10].
- 1 Oct 2013: MINDEF confirms a RM9 billion contract value in an amended letter of acceptance [11].
- Contract later stated as RM9.128 billion; agreed delivery of the six ships "between April 2019 and August 2023"; RM5.936 billion paid by 31 May 2019 [9].
- 4 Aug 2022: PAC report tabled: RM6.083 billion paid, no ship completed; RM1.4 billion used for other purposes including cost overruns; 63.8% paid for the first ship against 42.7% of work done; BNS finances described as weak; contract awarded by direct negotiation [5][6].
- 10 Aug 2022: Cabinet agrees to declassify the 2019 LCS reports and urges MACC to expedite [8]; 17 Aug 2022: declassified governance-committee report published [9].
- 11 Aug 2022: MACC completes investigation papers on several individuals and sends them to the AGC [7].
- 16 Aug 2022: former navy chief and former BNS managing director Ahmad Ramli Mohd Nor charged with three counts of CBT involving RM21.08 million; 12 Mar 2025: discharge not amounting to acquittal after a hospital report found him unfit to stand trial [16].
- 26 May 2023: sixth supplemental contract; five ships; contract price RM11.2 billion; payment terms moved "from milestone activity to progress of works" [12].
- January 2024: Ocean Sunshine Bhd, a wholly owned MOF Inc subsidiary, takes ownership; company renamed LUNAS [4][13].
- 24 Jun 2024: RM7.15 billion paid, 63.69% of RM11.22 billion [14][15].
- 28 Jan 2026: LCS 1 first sea-going; about RM8.3 billion (74%) spent; delivery December 2026 [1][3].
- 19 May 2026: LCS 1 in sea trials before sea acceptance trials; delivery December 2026 [2].
- October 2026: Auditor-General's Report 2/2026 readiness warning; delivery moved to April 2027; RM8.96 billion claimed [19][20][21].

## CONTRADICTIONS

1. **Amount spent (0154 vs official record).** RM5.936 billion at 31 May 2019 [9]; RM6.083 billion at October 2021 (PAC) [6]; RM7.15 billion at June 2024 [14][15]; about RM8.3 billion at January 2026 [1]; RM8.96 billion claimed at 25 Sep 2026 [21]. Authoritative at 0154's source date: MINDEF's RM8.3 billion. The headline and hook present the 2021 figure as current.
2. **Contract signing year (0154 vs 1805).** 0154: "contract signed 2011". 1805: "signed July 2014". Letter of award 16 Dec 2011; contract signed 17 Jul 2014 [10]. MINDEF's 2013 letter dates the contract period from 29 Dec 2011 [11]; the declassified report gives October 2013 to October 2023 [9]. 1805 is right on signing; 0154 should say "awarded 2011".
3. **Original contract value.** RM9 billion [11]; RM9.128 billion [9]; RM9.13 billion (0154, rounded). Consistent.
4. **DNAA disclosure (0154 vs 1805).** 1805 reports the March 2025 DNAA; 0154 reports only the 2022 charge.
5. **Payment basis.** 0154 says "progress-payment schedule weakly tied to milestones"; the 2023 contract revision describes the old basis as "milestone activity" and the new one as "progress of works" [12].

## SINCE PUBLICATION

- **Delivery of LCS 1 moved from December 2026 to April 2027**, with a liquidated ascertained damages notice to LUNAS (Defence Minister, 8 Oct 2026) [20]. Programme 80.72% complete against 89.91% planned at 25 Sep 2026; LUNAS has claimed RM8.96 billion, 79.82% of the RM11.227 billion contract [21].
- **Auditor-General's Report 2/2026**: LCS 1 may be handed over without full capability; full readiness depends on complementary contracts running to December 2035 [19].
- Together these make "the unresolved test is delivery" sharper, and the RM6.08 billion figure further out of date.
