# BRIEF: EPF Akaun Fleksibel withdrawals reached RM14.79 billion by 30 June 2025

**Slug:** epf-flexible-account-3-withdrawals-reach-rm1479b-i

**Source date:** 2026-04-08
**Backfill date: 2026-11-08**
**Brief status:** Source-list backfill. Issue cards unchanged.

## ISSUE

EPF restructured members' accounts on 11 May 2024, creating Akaun Fleksibel, from which members under 55 can withdraw at any time. In a written parliamentary reply reported on 14 August 2025, the Ministry of Finance said RM14.79 billion had been withdrawn by 4.63 million members (35% of 13.2 million members under 55) up to 30 June 2025; EPF's own report later put usage at about 37% of eligible members and RM16.6 billion by October 2025, mostly for essentials. The issue argues that whatever the label, unreplenished withdrawals widen the retirement gap, and that the scale signals household-finance stress.

## CONTRADICTIONS

1. **Cumulative special withdrawals.** MOF's figure of RM101 billion covers i-Lestari, i-Sinar and i-Citra only (to March 2022) [10]; the World Bank's RM145 billion covers four withdrawals, including the 2022 special withdrawal [11]. MOF is the more authoritative for the three schemes named on the card; both contradict "tens of billions".
2. **Withdrawal totals at different cut-offs.** RM12.2 billion, 31% of members under 55, at December 2024 (BNM, citing EPF) [2]; RM14.79 billion, 35%, at 30 June 2025 (MOF) [3]; RM16.6 billion, about 37%, at October 2025 (EPF report) [4, 5]; RM19.87 billion at 31 May 2026 (MOF) [12]. These are consistent snapshots of a rising total, not conflicts.
3. **Eligible base.** MOF uses 13.2 million members under 55 [3]; EPF's report uses 13.3 million eligible members [4]. Minor; different dates.

## SINCE PUBLICATION

- **RM19.87 billion by 31 May 2026.** MOF written reply of 9 Jul 2026: about RM19.87 billion withdrawn as of 31 May 2026; about 4.7 million members had less than RM50 in the account as at October 2025 [12]. The headline is explicitly dated "by 30 June 2025", so it is not wrong, but the total is now about a third higher.
