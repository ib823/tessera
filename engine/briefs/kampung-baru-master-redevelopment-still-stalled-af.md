## ISSUE

Kampong Bharu (Kampung Baru) in Kuala Lumpur was established in 1899 and recognised as a Malay Agricultural Settlement (MAS) by Selangor gazette on 12 January 1900. The Kampong Bharu Development Corporation (PKB), created by Act 733 in 2011 and operating since July 2012, coordinates redevelopment of a 300.73-acre (about 121.7 ha) area under a block-based 2040 master plan. PKB's own project list shows two completed projects and no block of the master plan delivered. The separate Kampung Sungai Baru redevelopment (328 properties, outside the MAS area) moved through land acquisition, compensation and court-ordered evictions in 2025.

## PERIOD

1899 (founding) to November 2026.

## CONTEXT (timeline with sources)

- 1899: Kampong Bharu established; 12 Jan 1900: recognised as an MAS reserve by Selangor gazette; 1965: 838 land titles issued to occupants [1].
- 2009 to 2015: successive redevelopment announcements (Puncak Baru 2009; mosque redevelopment 2011–2012; Legasi Kampung Baru 2015) and a 2019 buyout proposal costed at RM6 to 10 billion if cash-only [11].
- 25 Dec 2011: Royal Assent to the Kampong Bharu Development Corporation Act 2011 (Act 733), gazetted 30 Dec 2011 [2]; PKB began operations in July 2012 [1].
- 2014: comprehensive development master plan prepared (as described by a 2016 working paper) [10]. Current plan: Pelan Induk Pembangunan Kampong Bharu 2040, 44 development blocks, 38 inside the MAS area [4].
- Kampung Sungai Baru: 67% of owners consented at first (72% after an incentive package); the remaining owners' land became subject to the Land Acquisition Act; compensation to non-consenting owners paid February 2022 to January 2023 [7].
- 11 Sep 2025: evictions from 37 houses in Kampung Sungai Baru; 3 Oct 2025: High Court dismisses 23 residents' bid to set aside refusal of a stay of the writ of possession [9].
- 6 Oct 2025: Minister (Federal Territories) Zaliha Mustafa tells the Dewan Rakyat that overlapping ownership is "one of the biggest hurdles" for redeveloping Malay reserve land, and that Kampung Baru is an MAS area gazetted under the Land Enactment 1897, not land under the Malay Reservation Enactment [8].
- 10 Oct 2025: Minister's briefing to MPs: PKB boundary 300.73 acres (219.91 acres MAS plus 80.82 acres outside, including Kampung Sungai Baru); Sungai Baru involves 328 properties (264 flats, 64 terrace houses) [7].
- 4 Nov 2026: PKB's project page still lists two completed projects [3].

## ACTORS

- Perbadanan Pembangunan Kampong Bharu (PKB), statutory body under Act 733 [1][2].
- Minister in the Prime Minister's Department (Federal Territories) Zaliha Mustafa [7][8].
- Lembaga Pentadbiran MAS (MAS board), named in reporting [7].
- KL City Gateway Sdn Bhd, Kampung Sungai Baru developer [9].
- Landowners and heirs, defined by Act 733 s.20(2) as the "Kampong Bharu community" for advisory-council purposes [2].

## RELEVANT LAW

- Kampong Bharu Development Corporation Act 2011 (Act 733): s.2 defines "interest of the Malays" to include "the heritage, customs, culture and historical values, of the Malays in Kampong Bharu"; s.14 sets PKB's functions as implementing policy and acting as "principal co-ordinating body" for development; s.20 creates an advisory council of landowners and heirs [2].
- Land Acquisition Act 1960, s.3(1)(b)–(c): PKB's stated fallback where negotiation fails, with at least 50% negotiated consent [5].
- Land Enactment 1897 s.6 and the MAS (Kuala Lumpur) Rules 1950: cited by the Minister as the legal basis of the MAS [8]. Texts not opened in this backfill.

**3R note.** The ownership rule attached to MAS land is a race-adjacent legal claim. The 3R standard needs two independent primary sources. This backfill opened one primary that confirms MAS status (PKB [1]) and none that states the ownership restriction itself. To clear it, open the MAS (Kuala Lumpur) Rules 1950 or the Selangor gazette of 12 Jan 1900, plus the Hansard of 6 Oct 2025 (parlimen.gov.my was unreachable in this session). Until then the card should describe the land as MAS land "reserved as a Malay settlement" and attribute the ownership restriction to the Minister's statement.

## CONTRADICTIONS

1. **Area.** 300.73 acres administered by PKB, of which 219.91 acres MAS (Minister) [7]; "223-acre" MAS (2016 working paper) [10]; original 221.5 acres later reaffirmed at about 220 acres (Minister) [7]. The card's 121.7 ha equals the full PKB boundary, not the MAS land alone, while the card's sub-line places 18,372 residents "on the Malay Agricultural Settlement land". Authoritative: the Minister's 2025 figures.
2. **Founding date.** 1899 established; 1900 gazetted [1]. Consistent; the card uses 1899.
3. **Population.** Card 18,372 (attributed to a PKB 2010 census); working paper "approximately 18,000" (2010) [10]. The exact figure is untraced.
4. **Is Kampung Sungai Baru MAS land?** The Minister says it was never gazetted as MAS or Malay reserve land [7]; reporting in 2024 (not opened) disputed this. The card treats it as a separate sub-project, which is consistent with the Minister.
