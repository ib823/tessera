## ISSUE

BNM's Annual Report 2025 shows e-payments rising 25% to 18.4 billion transactions in 2025. The issue contrasts that growth with survey evidence that cash remains a daily habit, especially outside urban areas, and with BNM data showing that scam losses now come mainly from victims being manipulated into authorising payments. Its question is who is left without working alternatives as cash recedes.

## CONTRADICTIONS

- **NSRC call count.** Home Minister (via The Star and The Sun): 146,167 complaint calls in 2025 [16, 17]. BNM AR2025, Diagram 4 (source NSRC): 138,953 calls on online financial scams, 83,232 from scammed victims [3]. Different scopes; the minister's figure matches the card.
- **NSRC money figure.** Card: RM34.5M recovered. The Star and The Sun both give about RM34.05 million, described as prevented from transfer to syndicates [16, 17]. The Sun's exact figure (RM34,051,357.68) is authoritative over the card's rounding.
- **Unauthorised fraud trend.** FSR 1H2025 (16 Oct 2025): unauthorised scam cases curbed [6]. FSR 2H2025 (31 Mar 2026): reported fraud cases continued to rise, driven mainly by malware enabling unauthorised transfers [5, 18]. The later review is authoritative for the 2025 picture.
- **DuitNow QR 2024 volume.** AR2024 gives 870 million person-to-merchant transactions in 2024 [8]; AR2025 restates 2024 volume as 1.5 billion [1]. Likely a scope difference (AR2024 excludes person-to-person and cross-border). Not used in the cards.
- **DuitNow QR touchpoints.** BNM: almost three million [1]. PayNet: more than three million [15]. Different dates or definitions; the card's "nearly 3 million" follows BNM.
