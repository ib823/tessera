## ISSUE

Bank Negara Malaysia's Annual Report 2025 records 18.4 billion e-payment transactions in 2025 (538 per person) and RM831 billion in retail e-payment value. The issue argues that adoption is solved at the rails level (DuitNow QR is the national QR standard) while the e-money market is uneven: one large issuer (TNG Digital) is profitable, smaller issuers are not shown to be, East Malaysian merchant onboarding is said to lag, and consumer data flows rest on signup consent under the PDPA.

## CONTRADICTIONS

- **E-money issuer count.** The FATF/APG MER gives 48 e-money issuers as of December 2024 [3]. BNM's live Financial Sector Participants Directory lists 49 non-bank e-money issuers on 8 Nov 2026 [4]. Different dates; both authoritative for their dates. The card's 48 is correctly attributed to FATF/APG.
- **S Pay Global users.** No source gives 1.56M. Bernama (30 Mar 2026) gives 1.6M [12]. The card's figure is close but unsourced.
- **ReDI regional figures.** Bernama's 1 Jul 2025 headline carries only the 22,368 cumulative figure [11]; The Sun carries the minister's Jan to May 2025 breakdown [10]. The card combines the two bases. The Sun's breakdown is the authoritative reading of the regional numbers.
