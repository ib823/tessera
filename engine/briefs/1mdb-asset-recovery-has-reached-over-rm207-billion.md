## ISSUE

MACC chief commissioner Azam Baki said on 6 May 2026 that Malaysia had recovered RM31.3 billion, 74.5% of the RM42 billion misappropriated from 1MDB, as of 2025. The Ministry of Finance separately reported in October 2025 that settlements with Goldman Sachs, AmBank and JP Morgan (Switzerland) had brought in RM20.71 billion. Goldman's 2020 settlement with Malaysia was US$2.5 billion in cash plus a US$1.4 billion asset-recovery guarantee; the US Department of Justice had returned about US$1.4 billion by June 2024. Since publication the MOF has said that even with RM31.3 billion recovered the government still faces a net RM20.1 billion burden.

## PERIOD

July 2020 (Goldman settlement) to October 2026 (MOF written replies).

## CONTEXT (timeline with sources)

- 24 Jul 2020: MOF announces the Goldman Sachs settlement: US$3.9 billion (RM16.6 billion at the time), comprising US$2.5 billion cash and a guarantee of at least US$1.4 billion in asset recoveries [4].
- 22 Oct 2020: Goldman's Form 8-K: the firm "paid a total of $2.5 billion, in addition to providing a $1.4 billion asset recovery guarantee" to the Government of Malaysia; its US and other settlements total about US$2.6 billion after crediting [1].
- 26 Feb 2021: MOF announces AMMB Holdings' RM2.83 billion global settlement and describes the Goldman deal as RM15.8 billion (US$3.9 billion) [2].
- 13 Jun 2024: US DOJ returns another US$156 million, bringing the total it has returned to about US$1.4 billion; DOJ says more than US$4.5 billion was misappropriated from 1MDB [3].
- 1 Oct 2025: Azam Baki says over RM30 billion recovered; original losses about RM42 billion, higher with interest [8].
- 14 to 15 Oct 2025: MOF written reply (to Yeo Bee Yin): settlements with three financial institutions total RM20.71 billion (Bernama) or about RM20.8 billion (FMT); Goldman US$3.9 billion, AmBank RM2.83 billion, JP Morgan (Switzerland) RM1.4 billion [5][6].
- 27 Nov 2025: MOF written reply (to Wan Azizah): RM31.2 billion recovered for 1MDB and SRC International from December 2018 to 30 Sep 2025; recovered funds used only to repay 1MDB and SRC debts; 1MDB debt obligations of RM24.46 billion outstanding [7].
- 6 May 2026: Azam Baki: RM31.3 billion recovered, 74.5% of RM42 billion, as of 2025; yearly recoveries listed from 2019 [9][10].
- October 2026: MOF written replies: RM31.3 billion recovered; government has paid RM42.5 billion; RM20.1 billion net burden remains if nothing more is recovered [11].

## CONTRADICTIONS

1. **Settlement total from the three institutions.** RM20.71 billion (Bernama, MOF reply) [6]; about RM20.8 billion (FMT, same reply) [5]; RM20.73 billion (card, and the stub's slug "RM20.7 billion"). Authoritative: RM20.71 billion, the figure Bernama attributes to the MOF reply. The difference from FMT's rounding most likely comes from the ringgit value used for Goldman's US$3.9 billion.
2. **Goldman's ringgit value.** RM16.6 billion at signing (MOF/Bernama, July 2020) [4] against RM15.8 billion (MOF, February 2021) [2]. Exchange-rate difference; neither is in the cards.
3. **Total recovered.** RM31.2 billion for 1MDB and SRC to 30 Sep 2025 (MOF) [7] against RM31.3 billion for 1MDB as of 2025 (MACC) [9][10] and RM31.3 billion (MOF, October 2026) [11]. Different scopes and dates; the card blends them by calling MACC's RM42 billion "1MDB and SRC-linked".
4. **Loss baseline.** More than US$4.5 billion (DOJ, alleged) [3] against RM42 billion (MACC) [9][10]; MOF reports RM42.5 billion paid by the government and RM20.1 billion still to bear if nothing more is recovered (October 2026) [11]. The card already says comparisons depend on the baseline.
5. **Slug against card.** The stub's slug and old headline said "over RM20.7 billion"; the current card says RM31.3 billion. The slug is kept so existing links do not break.

## SINCE PUBLICATION

- **6 May 2026 MACC statement** (after the 8 Apr 2026 source date) is the source of the card's RM31.3 billion and 74.5% figures [9][10]; the card's "said in 2025" should read "said in May 2026".
- **October 2026 MOF written replies**: RM31.3 billion recovered; RM42.5 billion paid by the government as at 31 Aug 2026; the government "will still have to bear the remaining obligations of RM20.1 billion" if nothing more is recovered [11]. This bears on the reframe and view: the recovery ratio against misappropriated funds (74.5%) is not the same as the share of 1MDB's cost to the public that has been covered.
