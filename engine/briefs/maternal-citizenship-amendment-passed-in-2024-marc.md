# BRIEF: Maternal citizenship: amendment passed in 2024, settlement for earlier children in March 2025, commencement still pending

**Issue ID:** 1325

**Backfill date:** 2026-11-08

## ISSUE

Before the amendment, a child born abroad was a citizen by operation of law only if the **father** was a citizen [3]. The Constitution (Amendment) Bill 2024 extends this to children of citizen mothers. It passed the Dewan Rakyat 206–1 on 17 Oct 2024 [1] and the Dewan Negara on 3 Dec 2024 [2]. It applies prospectively only [1]. Pre-amendment children under 18 were covered by a consent order recorded before a five-member Federal Court bench on 10 Mar 2025, which ended the *Suriani Kempe* litigation begun in 2020 [6]. As of 14 Oct 2026 the amendment had not come into force: the government was awaiting an enforcement date [9].

## CONTRADICTIONS

- **Name and year of the Act.** Hansard and reporting refer to the Constitution (Amendment) Bill 2024 [1, 2, 6]. The UN CRC refers to the "Constitution (Amendment) Act, 2025" [5]. No gazette was opened, so the Act's formal year is unconfirmed. In March 2025 Malay Mail reported that it had not yet been gazetted [6]. The cards' "Bill 2024" is accurate for passage.
- **Whether the amendment is an unqualified gain.** LFL criticised the Bill's non-retroactivity clause [7]. The UN CRC urges repeal of three provisions in the same Act: ending citizenship by operation of law for children of permanent residents, cutting the age limit from 21 to 18, and revoking foreign wives' citizenship if the marriage ends within two years [5, para. 35(a)]. The cards present the amendment as a net closing of the gap and omit this.

## SINCE PUBLICATION

- **9 Jun 2026:** The government was in the "final phase" of fixing the start date [8].
- **14 Oct 2026:** The Home Minister said the government was ready and was waiting only for an enforcement date from the Yang di-Pertuan Agong [9]. The cards do not say the amendment is in force, but the view card ("closes the central injustice") reads as if the fix is operative.
