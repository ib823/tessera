## ISSUE

Establish which environmental powers would move from federal authorities to
Sarawak, under which instrument, from what date, and what changes for a resident
seeking project information, objecting to an approval, or reporting pollution.

The editorial value is an accountable map of responsibilities before and after
the instrument. Neither stronger nor weaker environmental protection follows
automatically from a transfer of administration.

as it stood at publication, its predecessor and any commencement/transition
provisions. A Radar timestamp is not the instrument's publication date.

## ACTORS TO VERIFY

- NRES and DOE: the federal policy/administration explanation and retained duties.
- Sarawak government and NREB: receiving functions, implementation dates and

  experience of notice, access and remedies; avoid treating one voice as
  representative of a whole community.
- Project proponents and businesses: compliance transition and overlapping
  approvals; distinguish administrative clarity from reduced standards.

## RELEVANT LAW — RETRIEVAL REQUIRED

- Exact November amendment: title, gazette number, making power, publication
  date, assent requirements if applicable, commencement and schedules.
- Instrument being amended, including all relevant intervening amendments.
- Current Environmental Quality Act 1974 and provisions establishing the
  particular approval/enforcement/exclusion or delegation mechanism.
- Current Sarawak natural-resources/environment legislation and subsidiary
  orders defining prescribed activities, approvals, public participation,
  enforcement, review and appeal.
- Federal Constitution provisions relevant to the specific allocation of
  powers. Do not equate authority over land with exclusive authority over every
  environmental function, or apply Peninsular planning rules to Sarawak.
