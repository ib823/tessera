## ISSUE

After the late-February 2026 war involving Iran, shipping through the Strait of Hormuz collapsed and Iran let through ships of countries it called friendly. Malaysia was not on Iran's first list. Seven Malaysian-owned vessels waited for clearance until Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim spoke to President Masoud Pezeshkian on 26 March; they then left in stages. The issue asks whether a trade-dependent country can keep relying on personal calls rather than standing arrangements such as naval presence.

## CONTRADICTIONS

- **Vessel owner name.** Card: "Sepadu". NST quoting the Foreign Minister: "Shapadu Corporation Sdn Bhd" [7]. NST is authoritative.
- **Subsidy estimate.** MOF 26 Mar: up to RM4 billion a month, petrol and diesel together [17]. MOF 15 Apr: about RM7 billion a month, RON95 about RM4 billion [18]. The later figure is authoritative for April onward; the card's RM4 billion is accurate only as the 26 March estimate.
- **Wisma Putra statement.** The Foreign Ministry's 7 April release could not be opened (the kln.gov.my site failed TLS verification from this environment). Its content is taken from The Star's report of it [13].
