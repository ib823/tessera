## ISSUE

On 11 March 2026 the Prime Minister said Cabinet had decided that government agencies, GLCs and GLICs would not hold Aidilfitri open houses, and that overseas travel by ministers and officials would be restricted, as a signal of careful spending during the Middle East conflict. The issue weighs the modest savings against fuel-subsidy costs of billions a month and lost revenue for caterers, and argues for published procurement and audited costs instead of an on-off switch.
