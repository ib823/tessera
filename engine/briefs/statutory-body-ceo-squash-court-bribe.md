# BRIEF — Federal statutory body CEO arrested over RM1m squash-court bribe

## ISSUE
On 13 April 2026 the Malaysian Anti-Corruption Commission (MACC) detained the chief
executive officer of a federal statutory body over an alleged bribe to approve a
contractor's payment claims on a RM7 million squash-court complex. The alleged
mechanism is the editorial core: the bribe was not to *win* the contract but to
*release payment claims* on a contract already awarded — graft at the
claims-approval gate, where a single signatory controls disbursement. Small headline
sum (RM1m solicited; RM600k allegedly received) but a structural pattern: statutory
bodies sit outside the tightest federal procurement scrutiny, and the
payment-certification step is a recurring corruption choke point.

**Accuracy posture:** the suspect has NOT been charged as at the brief date — he was
remanded to assist investigation. The individual and the specific statutory body are
NOT named by MACC. T4A must mirror that: no naming, no guilt assertion, presumption
of innocence, "alleged" throughout. A social-media claim linking the case to a named
MP's office circulated and was **denied**; it is unverified and must be EXCLUDED
(defamation + 3R-irrelevant + unverified).

## PERIOD
- **Before Hari Raya Aidilfitri (late Mar 2026):** suspect allegedly received
  RM500,000 as advance.
- **13 Apr 2026, ~7:20pm:** detained at a shopping complex in Bukit Jalil, KL, after
  allegedly receiving a further RM100,000 cash from the contractor.
- **14 Apr 2026:** remanded five days (to 18 Apr) by magistrate Ezrene Zakaria,
  Putrajaya Magistrates' Court.
- **15 Apr 2026:** named MP publicly denies any link to the case (unverified
  third-party allegation — excluded from the issue).

## ACTORS
- **The suspect** — CEO of an (un-named) federal statutory body, male, in his 40s.
  Allegedly the payment-approval authority for the project. NOT named, NOT charged.
- **MACC (Suruhanjaya Pencegahan Rasuah Malaysia, SPRM)** — arresting and
  investigating agency. Announced via statement, 14 Apr 2026.
- **The contractor** — alleged bribe-giver on the squash-court project. Not named.
- **Magistrate Ezrene Zakaria** — issued the five-day remand, Putrajaya.

## RELEVANT LAW
- **MACC Act 2009, s.16/s.17(a)** — soliciting/accepting gratification as an agent.
- **MACC Act 2009, s.23** — abuse of position/office by a public officer.
- **MACC Act 2009, s.36** — remand-to-assist-investigation power.
- **Government procurement framework** — Treasury Instructions / 1PP (Perolehan
  Kerajaan); statutory bodies operate under their own enabling Acts and board
  governance, with payment certification a controlled but single-signatory-vulnerable
  step.
- Presumption of innocence: Federal Constitution Art. 5; CPC remand ≠ charge.
