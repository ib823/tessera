## ISSUE

PAS-led Perikatan Nasional (PN) governments hold Kelantan, Terengganu and Kedah, and PN holds Perlis. After the Federal
Court struck down 16 Kelantan Syariah criminal provisions in *Nik Elin* (9 Feb 2024), the Kelantan assembly voted to
re-enact them and Kedah amended its state constitution to entrench its Syariah courts. The cards argue that the
constitutional line (State List item 1, Article 121(1A), the pith-and-substance test) already defines how far Islamic law
can go, and that attitude surveys show broad support for sharia in principle but narrower support for hudud.

## CONTRADICTIONS

- **Perlis leadership.** The cards (source date 2026-04-08) describe PAS as anchoring the Perlis government. The
  reporting of 29 Dec 2025 and 7 Jan 2026 (7, 8) shows Bersatu holding the MB post and PAS out of the exco. The
  later reporting is authoritative because it is dated and specific.
- **Scope of Syariah jurisdiction.** The cards say "primarily personal and family law". The Ninth Schedule text (1) also
  lists offences against the precepts of Islam and other religious matters. The Constitution text governs.
- **Hudud measures.** Pew (16) asks about support for specific punishments among sharia supporters, and Merdeka (18)
  asks about readiness. They are not the same question, so "narrower support" compares unlike measures.
