## ISSUE
The EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR, Regulation (EU) 2023/1115) requires that palm oil and six other
commodities entering the EU be proven free of deforestation (no land cleared after 31 December 2020) and
geolocated to the plot of origin. After two postponements it applies from 30 December 2026 for large and
medium operators and 30 June 2027 for micro and small enterprises. Malaysia's roughly 450,000 oil-palm
smallholders, who work about 27% of the country's 5.7 million hectares, are the weakest link in plot-level
traceability. Two under-covered points: (1) the EU now takes only a small slice of Malaysia's palm exports,
so the bigger stake is precedent, as EUDR-style traceability becomes a global buyer norm; (2) Malaysia made
its own standard, MSPO, mandatory in 2020, and the EU has recognised it as a route to compliance, yet about
a quarter of independent smallholders were still uncertified, the group most exposed to the deadline.

## CLAIMS DROPPED / SOFTENED FOR ACCURACY
- **EU palm trade value contradiction handled, not hidden.** One source gives palm-related exports to the EU
  as "less than €2 billion," another calls the EU "the single largest structured market for palm oil ...
  US$6.6 billion in 2023." These are not reconcilable at face value (different baskets/definitions). Cards
  avoid a single hard figure and say the EU takes "a small and shrinking slice," which both sources support
  directionally relative to India/China. Flag for Stage 3 to resolve.
- Did NOT frame EUDR as simple "EU discrimination" (a common Malaysian-government line) nor as "Malaysia
  destroys forests." Framed as a burden-distribution and precedent story: who can prove provenance and who cannot.
- "Locked out of the premium tier" is framed as a market consequence, not a certainty for any named firm.

## KEY STATISTICS
- EUDR: applies 30 Dec 2026 (large/medium), 30 June 2027 (micro/small); cutoff 31 Dec 2020; geolocation required.
- Smallholders: ~450,000; ~27% of 5.7M ha oil palm. Independent licensed smallholders: 210,891 (76.9% MSPO-certified).
- MSPO: mandatory 2020; 86.5% of area (4.89M ha) certified by Dec 2024.
- EU palm-related imports from Malaysia: under €2B (of €24B total goods) — carry as "small slice," see contradictions.
