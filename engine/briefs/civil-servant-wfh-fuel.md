# BRIEF: Work from home for federal civil servants, introduced to save fuel during the Hormuz crisis

**Issue ID:** 1954
**Slug:** civil-servant-wfh-fuel (same slug as the existing stage files `engine/output/civil-servant-wfh-fuel-stage*.json`)
**Source date:** 2026-05-17
**Backfill date: 2026-11-08**
**Brief type:** source-list backfill. Issue cards unchanged.

## ISSUE

In a special address on 26 March 2026 the Prime Minister said civil servants would be able to work from home in phases, in the same speech that cut the BUDI95 petrol quota. A Public Service Department circular dated 2 April set the rules from 15 April: eligible locations, a commute threshold of 8 km, set WFH days, exclusions for frontline services, hourly geolocation attendance through SPOT-Me, and KPI monitoring. The issue asks whether a crisis measure can become a lasting reform measured by output rather than attendance.

## CONTRADICTIONS

- **Commute rule.** Context says "an 8 km commute threshold"; fact 1 says "within an 8 km commute". Bernama's report of the circular says more than eight kilometres [8]. Bernama, quoting the circular, is authoritative.
- **Scope of the 1 April announcement.** Bernama on 1 April reported WFH for "the public sector and government-linked companies" [7]; the 2 April PSD circular as reported on 14 April covers federal public servants [8]. GLC arrangements are outside the circular.
