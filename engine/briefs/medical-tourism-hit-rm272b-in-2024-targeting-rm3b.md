# BRIEF: Medical tourism earned RM2.72 billion in 2024; the 2030 target is RM12 billion while public specialist shortages persist

**Issue ID:** 1445
**Slug:** medical-tourism-hit-rm272b-in-2024-targeting-rm3b
**Source date:** 2026-04-08
**Backfill date: 2026-11-08**
**Brief status:** Source-list backfill. Replaces the 2026-04-08 stub. Issue cards unchanged; findings below are for the editor.

## ISSUE

The Malaysia Healthcare Travel Council (MHTC) reported 1.6 million healthcare travellers and RM2.72 billion in revenue for 2024, and set a goal of RM12 billion in industry revenue by 2030. The 2025 target was RM3 billion; preliminary MHTC figures released in April 2026 show 2025 ended at about 1.84 million travellers and RM3.34 billion, above target. Health Ministry data show a public-sector specialist shortfall of about 10,800 in 2025 and more than 13,000 against 2030 needs. The issue asks whether specialist supply can grow with the export.

## PERIOD

2024 to May 2026.

## CONTEXT (timeline with sources)

- 7 Jul 2025: MOH briefing data: public sector short of 10,798 specialists in 2025 (needs 17,393, has 7,576) [10].
- 24 Jul 2025: MHTC launches Malaysia Year of Medical Tourism 2026; 2024: 1.6 million travellers (+14%), RM2.72 billion revenue (+21%); goal RM12 billion by 2030; four Flagship Medical Tourism Hospital (FMTH) finalists named [1].
- 6 Aug 2025: MHTC expects about RM3 billion in 2025 and aims for over RM12 billion by 2030 [6].
- 9 Dec 2025: Island Hospital, Penang named the first FMTH; the other finalists (IJN, Mahkota Medical Centre, Subang Jaya Medical Centre) receive special awards; 2025 target stated as RM3 billion from 2.5 million travellers [2][5].
- 2 Mar 2026: Health Minister's written reply in the Dewan Negara (24 Feb 2026): MOH needs 22,435 specialists by 2030 against 9,040 in service [11].
- 19 to 21 Apr 2026: early figures for 2025: 1.84 million travellers and about RM3.34 billion, "as of 31 March 2026" (MHTC via Bernama) [7]; private hospitals took 95% of 2025 healthcare travel revenue; industry revenue "projected to hit RM7 billion by 2030" [8].
- 18 May 2026: 2025 revenue reported as RM3.35 billion, up 23.2%, with about 1.85 million patients [9]; MHTC's site shows RM3.35 billion for 2025 [3].

## ACTORS

- Malaysia Healthcare Travel Council (MHTC), the Health Ministry's agency for healthcare travel [1][2][3].
- Ministry of Health and Health Minister Dzulkefly Ahmad [5][11].
- FMTH finalists: Island Hospital Penang (winner), Institut Jantung Negara, Mahkota Medical Centre, Subang Jaya Medical Centre [1][2].
- Government of Maharashtra (India), for the charitable-hospital comparison [4].

## RELEVANT LAW

- Maharashtra Public Trusts Act, s.41AA: the Charity Commissioner and State Government may direct state-aided public trusts with annual expenditure above Rs 5 lakh to reserve 10% of operational beds free for indigent patients and 10% at concessional rates for weaker sections; scheme approved by the Bombay High Court in Writ Petition (PIL) No. 3132/2004 [4].
- Private Healthcare Facilities and Services Act 1998 (Act 586), Malaysia: not opened in this backfill (the MOH copy returned HTTP 403), so the card's negative claim about Malaysian rules is unverified.

## CONTRADICTIONS

1. **2030 revenue figure.** MHTC: RM12 billion by 2030 [1][3][6]. Malay Mail (April 2026): healthcare travel revenue "projected to hit RM7 billion by 2030" [8]. These may measure different things (target against projection), but the gap is large. The card's RM12 billion is MHTC's official target and is authoritative for "target".
2. **2024 traveller count.** 1.6 million (MHTC) [1][2] against 1.52 million (Focus Malaysia, citing MHTC) [6]. MHTC's own release is authoritative.
3. **2025 results.** 1.84 million and RM3.34 billion (preliminary, as of 31 Mar 2026) [7] against 1.85 million and RM3.35 billion (May 2026) [9] and RM3.35 billion on MHTC's site [3]. Later figures supersede the preliminary ones.
4. **Specialist shortfall.** 10,798 in 2025 (MOH briefing slides, June 2025) [10] against 13,395 relative to 2030 needs (written reply, February 2026) [11]. Different baselines (current need against 2030 need), both from MOH.

## SINCE PUBLICATION

- MHTC's 2025 figures settled at about RM3.35 billion and 1.85 million travellers [3][9]. The 2025 target (RM3 billion) was exceeded. The card's "early-2025 figures" wording should be corrected and the 2025 outcome added.
- An industry projection of RM7 billion by 2030 was published on 19 Apr 2026, eleven days after the source date [8]; the editor may want to note it next to MHTC's RM12 billion target.
