## ISSUE

The MRT3 Circle Line was scrapped by the Mahathir government on 30 May 2018 and approved again by the Ismail Sabri Cabinet on 4 March 2022. In the re-tabled Budget 2023 speech (24 February 2023) the government said the project was estimated at RM68 billion in 2018 and RM50 billion when procurement began in 2022, and that it was confident of bringing the cost below RM45 billion. The Transport Minister approved the final railway scheme on 17 July 2025; land acquisition is targeted to finish by end-2026 and construction may start in 2027. The RM45 billion figure is a stated target, not a statutory cap.

## PERIOD

May 2018 to November 2026.

## CONTEXT (timeline with sources)

- 30 May 2018: Prime Minister Mahathir Mohamad says the government has decided to scrap MRT3 "for now" [6].
- 4 Mar 2022: Cabinet agrees to implement MRT3 (Prime Minister Ismail Sabri's media statement) [2]; Transport Minister confirms the 4 March approval in Parliament [7].
- 15 Mar 2022: MRT Corp estimates construction at about RM31 billion, about RM8.4 billion for land and about RM8 billion for interest; full operation targeted for 2030 [8][9].
- 24 Feb 2023: Budget 2023 speech, para. 185: estimated at RM68 billion in 2018, RM50 billion when procurement began in 2022; government confident of savings "sekurang-kurangnya di bawah 45 bilion ringgit" (at least below RM45 billion) [1].
- 19 Sep 2024: Transparency International Malaysia calls for an Independent Expert Monitor and integrity pact, with consultation "before the tender documents are finalised" [5].
- 26 Sep 2024: reporting cites operations beginning 2032 [14].
- 18 Nov 2024: MRT Corp CEO says construction may only start in 2027 after land acquisition [13].
- 17 Jul 2025: Transport Minister Loke Siew Fook formally approves and signs the Final Railway Scheme; 51 km orbital line, 10 interchange stations; land lots cut from 1,012 to 690; land acquisition targeted for completion by end-2026 [4][10][11].
- 18 Jul 2025: an unnamed research house, quoted by Focus Malaysia, puts construction at about RM31 billion and land at RM6 to 7 billion, with construction possibly from 2027 [12].
- 15 Oct 2026: the Budget 2027 speech contains no MRT3 passage (checked) [3].

## CONTRADICTIONS

1. **Land acquisition cost.** About RM8.4 billion (MRT Corp, March 2022) [8][9] against RM6 to 7 billion (unnamed research house, July 2025, after the land lots were cut from 1,012 to 690) [12][4]. The 2025 figure is plausibly lower because fewer lots are needed, but it is an analyst estimate, not an MRT Corp or MOF figure. The card should say "estimated", not "allocation".
2. **Operational target.** Full operation by 2030 (2022) [7][8][9] against operations from 2032 (2024 reporting) [14]. The later date reflects the 2027 construction start [13].
3. **Line length.** 51 km (MRT Corp) [4] against 51.6 km (Malay Mail) [11]. Minor.
4. **Cost range in 2022.** RM31 billion to RM50 billion (MRT Corp, March 2022) [8] against RM50 billion as the 2022 procurement estimate (Budget 2023 speech) [1]. The speech is authoritative for the MOF's own baseline.
5. **The 2018 RM68 billion figure.** Taken from the Budget 2023 speech [1]. A search-result headline (EdgeProp, 2018) described the scrapped project as "RM40b"; that article was not opened and is not relied on. The editor may want to note that the RM68 billion baseline is the government's own 2023 characterisation of the 2018 estimate.
