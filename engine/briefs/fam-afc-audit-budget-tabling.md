# BRIEF — AFC audit finds FAM did not table its budget for Congress approval for most of a decade

**Slug:** fam-afc-audit-budget-tabling
**Proposed issue ID:** 2009
**Radar provenance:** silence-watch S11 (silence=1.00, importance=0.61, age 1.0d), 2026-06-07 scan; develop-list pick A1
**Source date:** 2026-06-07

## ISSUE
An Asian Football Confederation (AFC) governance audit of the Football Association of
Malaysia (FAM), presented at FAM's Extraordinary Congress on 4 June 2026, found that
FAM failed to table its annual budget for **Congress approval** since 2016 — a
requirement under its own statutes — and scored FAM at "Level 2 (Pre-Intermediate)"
across most areas, with Finance at 1.2/5 and Governance and Legal at 1.3/5. Members,
in other words, were not given the single document that lets them hold the
association to account, for the better part of a decade. Former president Hamidin
Mohd Amin conceded the lapse may have been "negligence." The audit lands on a body
already a year deep in crisis: in September 2025 FIFA found FAM used forged
naturalisation documents for seven players, fined it CHF 350,000, and the entire
2025–2029 executive committee resigned en masse in January 2026.

**The editorial core:** the budget is the members' one instrument of oversight. The
audit shows the oversight switch was off — and the full audit report is being shown
only in part, which an MP says invites "an adverse inference." Anger-at-process, not
sadness; the relatability is that this is *football*, the institution every Malaysian
household has an opinion on.

**Accuracy posture (READ BEFORE WRITING ANY CARD):** there are TWO distinct claims
that must NOT be collapsed.
1. **AFC finding (supported):** FAM did not table its annual **budget** for **Congress
   approval** as its statutes require, dating back to 2016.
2. **Separate claim FAM DENIES:** that it failed to table its **audited accounts /
   financial reports** since 2016. FAM says it *did* present financial reports at
   every annual congress, including the 2025 accounts at the 4 June EGM, and that its
   statutes did **not** require the annual budget to be tabled at congress between
   2020 and 2022 (budgets were reviewed by the finance & management committee and at
   exco level instead).
   → T4A must frame this as **budget-for-Congress-approval**, never as
   "hid its accounts" or "no audited accounts for 10 years." The "10 years" line
   belongs to Hamidin as his characterisation, attributed, not asserted flatly.

## RELEVANT FRAMEWORK / "LAW"
- **FAM Statutes** — require the annual budget to be tabled for Congress approval (the
  provision the audit says was not followed since 2016). The 94 AFC-proposed amendments
  revise these statutes.
- **FIFA Statutes / FIFA Governance Regulations** — member associations must run to
  FIFA/AFC governance standards; basis for the AFC audit and the Art. 22
  (forged/falsified documents) finding.
- **No Malaysian statute is the hook** — FAM is a society/association, not a government
  body. Frame this as association governance and public-money accountability (govt
  grants), NOT as a breach of Malaysian law. Do not imply criminality.
