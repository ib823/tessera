## ISSUE

The Ministry of Home Affairs announced on 14 January 2026 a new expatriate employment policy, approved by Cabinet on 17 October 2025, effective 1 June 2026: the Employment Pass Category I minimum salary rises from RM10,000 to RM20,000, Category II to RM10,000–RM19,999 and Category III to RM5,000–RM9,999 (RM7,000 for manufacturing and manufacturing-related services), with tenure caps of 10, 10 and 5 years and replacement (succession) plans for Categories II and III. The official aim is to focus expatriate hiring on high-impact expertise. The issue argues that raising the price of foreign hires is separate from building the local talent pipeline.

## CONTRADICTIONS

1. **SGD to RM conversion.** The card uses about RM3.30 per SGD; BNM's published rate on the source date is about RM3.09–3.13 [9]. BNM is authoritative.
2. **Semiconductor talent target wording.** Card: "60,000 workers"; MIDA release quoting the national target: "60,000 highly skilled engineers" [5].

## SINCE PUBLICATION

- **Succession-plan requirement deferred.** ESD, 26 May 2026: the revised salary policy takes effect on 1 Jun 2026, but "the succession plan requirement will only take effect from 1 January 2027 onwards" [4]. The context and hook sub present replacement plans as applying from 1 June 2026.
- **Singapore floor rising.** MOM's eligibility page now lists higher floors for new applications from 1 Jan 2027 (SGD6,000 non-finance, SGD6,600 finance, rising with age) [7]. The date MOM announced this was not established in this pass, so it may predate the source date.

## CORRECTION (2026-11-09)

Edition 3 of issue 1956:

- **Regional card:** the ringgit conversions were wrong (they implied about RM3.30 per SGD). At BNM's 30 Mar 2026 rate (RM3.0913 buying, RM3.1333 selling) [9]: SGD5,600 is about RM17,400 (range RM17,311–17,546) and SGD6,200 about RM19,300 (range RM19,166–19,426), so both Singapore entry floors sit below RM20,000. The card now says these floors apply at age 23 and under, rising to SGD10,700 outside finance at 45 and over (about RM33,000) [7].
- **Context, hook sub:** ESD deferred the succession (replacement) plan requirement to 1 Jan 2027 (Announcement 278, 26 May 2026) [4]. The salary floor took effect on 1 Jun 2026 as planned [4].
- **Economic card:** "60,000 workers" corrected to "60,000 highly skilled engineers" under the National Semiconductor Strategy [5]. The unverified "6,600 E&E graduates per year" is removed.
- **Not changed:** Singapore's announced 2027 floors (date of announcement not established) are not added.
