# BRIEF: Malaysia's e-invoice mandate softened three times in five months after the Sabah election

## ISSUE

A week after Pakatan Harapan's heavy defeat in the 29 November 2025 Sabah state election, the Prime Minister raised the e-invoice exemption threshold from RM500,000 to RM1 million. The penalty-free transition for firms with RM1 million to RM5 million turnover was then extended to end-2026 (5 January 2026) and to end-2027 (20 April 2026). The issue sets this against LHDN data showing high compliance and undeclared income found through e-invoice checks, and against estimates of the shadow economy.

## CONTRADICTIONS

- **Shadow economy size.** Government (Ahmad Maslan, 2023): 21% of GDP [12]. KRI: informal sector 4.0-7.8% (2019), shadow economy about 23.0% by extrapolation [13]. World Economics: 24.5% now [14]. The figures measure different things (informal sector versus shadow economy); none is an official DOSM statistic. The card's "7% to 26.6%" range matches none of them exactly.
- **E-invoice totals.** Bernama, 1 Jan 2026, cites "106,000+ registered taxpayers" as of 4 Nov 2025 [15]; HASiL gives 111,600 by 22 Dec 2025 [2]. Different dates; consistent.

## SINCE PUBLICATION

- **A fourth softening.** HASiL, 30 Aug 2026: following the PM's National Day address, the e-invoice implementation threshold rose from RM1 million to RM3 million from 1 Sep 2026; more than 1.1 million traders are now exempt [5]. The headline count ("three times in five months") is accurate for the source date but no longer the full record.
- HASiL also reported 1.84 billion e-invoices from 265,379 taxpayers by 30 Aug 2026 [5], superseding the 820.5 million figure as the latest total.
