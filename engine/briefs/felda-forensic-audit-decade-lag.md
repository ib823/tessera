## ISSUE

On 2 September 2026 the MACC announced seven investigation papers into Felda and Felda Investment
Corporation (FIC) covering purchases and investments made between 2010 and 2020. In the fortnight
before, the Deputy Prime Minister called for a forensic audit of Felda (23 Aug) and the Prime
Minister said the proposal would go to Cabinet (25 Aug).

Mainstream coverage carries all of this as new. It is not.

In 2019 the Economic Affairs Ministry appointed **Ernst & Young Advisory Services Sdn Bhd** to run a
forensic study of eight significant Felda/FIC purchases and investments. EY's report, dated 4 April
2019, was published in full as **Lampiran 1 of the FELDA White Paper (Kertas Perintah 6 Tahun 2019,
10 April 2019)** and laid before Parliament. It found RM4.38 billion committed across the eight
deals, roughly half of it already impaired, governance failure throughout, and explicit "elements of
non-compliance and misconduct".

**Seven of the eight deals EY audited in 2019 are the seven MACC opened papers on in 2026.** Only the
Indonesian plantation stake (PT Eagle High Plantations) is absent from MACC's list. One of the seven,
Park City Grand Plaza Kensington, is not even a new file: MACC opened it in 2017 and it remains open,
in mutual legal assistance.

The developable core is therefore **not** "Felda was looted" (documented since 2019) and **not** "MACC
is finally acting" (it acted in 2017). It is the **interval**: the state commissioned a forensic
audit, published it in Parliament, and is now, seven years later, commissioning another forensic
audit and opening investigation papers into the same deals.

The honest counter-argument, which must sit on a fact card and not in a footnote: **the state did
act.** Felda and FIC filed a RM2 billion civil suit in November 2019, and former chairman Mohd Isa
Abdul Samad was convicted, acquitted, re-convicted and had his final review dismissed on 26 June
2026. Neither track is fast, and neither covers most of the eight deals, but "nothing happened" is
false and must not be written.

Anger-at-process, and bipartisan: the deals are from the BN era, the White Paper is PH's, and the
2026 reopening is under a unity government whose Deputy Prime Minister (UMNO president) is the one
calling for the new audit.

## KEY STATISTICS

Every figure below is from the White Paper/EY annex [S1] unless marked.

- **RM4,382 million** total investment value across the eight deals (Paparan 1, "Jumlah").
- **RM2,116 million** impairment per audited financial statements; **RM92 million** further potential
  impairment; **RM2,174 million** carrying value after impairment (Paparan 1).
- EY 1.3.1: impairment "lebih kurang 50% daripada nilai pelaburan asal yang berjumlah RM4.4 bilion,
  iaitu mencecah RM2.2 bilion" — and this is **before** revaluing the three UK assets and GBHSB.
- EY 1.3.2 (**accounting lag**): some impairments were not booked in the year they should have been.
  Paparan 1 shows DASB impaired in 2017 against a "should have been" year of 2012; FWC impaired 2017
  against 2015; GBH not impaired at all against 2014.
- Per-deal investment (Paparan 1, RM million): EHP 2,215 (impaired 1,576); DASB 148 (146);
  GBH 86 (nil); GPSA 528 (nil); FWC 191 (191); GPK 331 (203); DEX 883 (nil).
- KLVC: no payment made to date, but Felda bound to rental commitments of **at least RM1.5 billion
  over 20 years**; land transferred to third parties without Felda's knowledge.
- Felda group: loss RM2.0b (2013) against profit RM5.9b (2012), rising to **RM4.9b loss (2017)**;
  liabilities RM1.2b (2007) to **RM14.4b (2017)**; average income RM3.1b/year over 2007–2011.
- Auditor-General [S4]: Felda **RM1b net loss (2022)**, against RM545m (2021); **RM8.66b** owed to
  external institutions; grants RM200m (2022) against RM342m (2021).
- Isa Samad: nine counts, **RM3.09 million**, six years' jail, **RM15.45 million** fine [S6], [S10].
- Civil suit: **RM2 billion**, 21 defendants, filed November 2019 [S17].

## CONTRADICTIONS

- **C1 — White Paper date.** Cover reads 10 April 2019 (Kertas Perintah 6 Tahun 2019); several reports
  say tabled 11 April 2019 by Azmin Ali. *Resolution:* the document's own date is authoritative for
  the document; cards say "April 2019".
- **C2 — Company name.** White Paper 1.1.1 reads "**Dataran** Aras Sdn Bhd"; September 2026 reports
  read "**Tataran** Aras Sdn Bhd". *Resolution:* prefer the primary document; do not name this company
  on any card.
- **C3 — Six or seven papers.** The Edge reports six; FMT reports seven. *Resolution:* six new (opened
  on information received 24 Aug) plus one pre-existing Park City Grand Plaza file under MLA = seven.
  Use seven and state the composition.
- **C4 — The August 2026 hotel is unidentified.** Reported as bought at GBP160m and offered at GBP100m.
  No report names the asset. EY's DEX cost GBP167.76m actual (GBP163m as presented to the FIC board),
  GPSA was about RM528m, GPK was GBP60m. **No exact match.** *Resolution:* **do not identify the asset
  and do not link it to any audited deal on any card.** Treat as an unresolved gap.
- **C5 — Which loss number.** White Paper: RM4.9b group loss (2017). AG: RM1b net loss (2022). Anwar:
  "losses approaching RM10b" (27 Jul 2026), reported variously as losses and as debt. *Resolution:*
  use the White Paper for 2017 and the AG for 2022. **Do not put Anwar's RM10b on a card** — the basis
  is reported inconsistently and it would be an unverified conflation.
- **C6 — Kickback amount.** RM3 million (Star, CoA) vs RM3.09 million (Malay Mail, Bernama).
  *Resolution:* RM3.09m is the more precise; cards do not need the figure.
