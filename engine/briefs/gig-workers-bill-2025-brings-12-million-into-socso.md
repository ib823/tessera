# BRIEF: Gig Workers Act 872 extends SOCSO self-employment cover; EPF retirement still missing

**Slug:** gig-workers-bill-2025-brings-12-million-into-socso

## ISSUE

The Gig Workers Bill 2025 passed the Dewan Rakyat on 28 August 2025 and the Dewan Negara on 9 September 2025, and became the Gig Workers Act 2025 (Act 872), gazetted 31 December 2025 and in force from 31 March 2026. It makes platform providers register gig workers with PERKESO and deduct their social security contributions (1.25% per task, per later reporting), but sets no EPF retirement contribution. The issue argues that the "no social protection" framing is now narrower: the remaining gap is retirement adequacy, which platforms still do not fund.

## CONTRADICTIONS

1. **i-Saraan coverage share, 2022 (used on fact card 2).** World Bank PRWP 10629 (Dec 2023): 10.7% of self-employed (own-account plus unpaid family workers) [6]. World Bank, *Informal Employment in Malaysia* (Feb 2024): 12.6% on the same stated definition [5]. Arithmetic from EPF's 291,743 participants [5, Fig. 9] and DOSM's 2.86 million own-account workers in December 2022 gives about 10.2% of own-account workers alone, or about 8.7% if about 0.49 million unpaid family workers are added. Neither World Bank figure can be reproduced from the DOSM releases opened. Judgement: the later report is not clearly better; the card should either cite the source by name ("World Bank, 2023") or give the range.
2. **Informal employment.** The card pairs Q1 2025 labour-force figures [3] with a 2023 informal-employment count [4]. DOSM's 2025 survey (released 9 Sep 2026) puts 2023 informal employment at 3,450.5 thousand and 2025 at 3,277.1 thousand [14].
3. **Gig-worker count.** "Over 1.2 million" (KESUMA, Sep 2025) [12]; about 1.64 million (Minister, Mar 2026) [8]; about 1.6 million, or 3.3 million on a broad own-account definition (ISIS, Jul 2026) [7]; about 600,000 e-hailing and p-hailing drivers (Budget 2027 speech) [13]. These measure different populations; the card correctly says "Methodologies differ."

## SINCE PUBLICATION

- **Informal employment updated.** DOSM's *Informal Sector and Informal Employment Survey Report 2025* (9 Sep 2026): informal employment fell to 3.28 million in 2025 (rate 19.8% of employment, from 21.8% in 2023) [14]. The card's 3.45 million is now two survey rounds old.
- **Budget 2027 (9 Oct 2026).** Gig Workers Consultative Council negotiating a minimum income rate and minimum social-protection standards, expected early 2027; Government–Grab RM160 million package from 2027; SOCSO matching incentive 35% (50% if the platform contributes); EPF matching incentive up to RM600 a year (lifetime RM6,000) for e-hailing and p-hailing drivers [13]. Retirement saving remains voluntary; there is still no mandatory EPF contribution. The view card's "Phase 2" question is partly answered: voluntary matching, not mandatory coverage.
