## ISSUE
Malaysia's total fertility rate (TFR) has stayed below the 2.1 replacement level continuously since 2013;
it was 1.6 in 2024. The number of live births in 2024 was the fewest in more than four decades. The
under-reported structural fact: the statistics department's own projection has the population peaking at
42.38 million in 2059, then declining to 41.43 million by 2070 — a turning point almost never reported.
Coverage treats each year's birth drop as a fresh surprise; the decade-long below-replacement trend and the
projected peak are the real story. The same projection drives a fast-ageing population, the engine behind the
pension and healthcare pressure analysed in the companion issue.
