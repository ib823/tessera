# BRIEF: Suaram's reported 20–28% release-without-charge rate for SOSMA detainees, against an official 6,892 detentions since 2012

**Issue ID:** 1241
**Slug:** suaram-up-to-28-of-sosma-detainees-released-withou
**Source date:** 2026-04-08
**Backfill date:** 2026-11-08
**Brief status:** Source-list backfill. Issue cards unchanged; findings below are for the editor.

## ISSUE

Section 4(5) of the Security Offences (Special Measures) Act 2012 (SOSMA) lets a police officer of Superintendent rank or above extend pre-charge detention "for a period of not more than twenty-eight days, for the purpose of investigation" [1, 2]. At its April 2026 launch of the Malaysia Human Rights Report 2025, Suaram said about 20–28% of SOSMA detainees are later released without charge [5, 6]. The 6,892 figure the cards attribute to Suaram matches the Home Minister's March 2026 Dewan Negara written reply on detentions from 2012 to 2025 [7], so it is an official count that Suaram cited, not Suaram's own tally.

Scope note: this is a narrow, data-attribution issue. Suaram's full 2025 report (the source of the 20–28% range) and the Dewan Negara written reply could not be opened: Suaram's launch page links no PDF [8], and the Parliament document repository returned errors throughout. The count therefore rests on two named outlets reporting the same figures [5, 6, 7]. Fewer than 8 primary sources bear on these claims; none were padded in.

## CONTRADICTIONS

- **Period behind 6,892.** FMT (7 Apr 2026) reports Suaram's 6,892 as arrests "between 2018 and 2023" [5]. Malaysiakini (7 Apr 2026) reports "since 2012" [6]. The Home Minister's Dewan Negara written reply, reported by FMT on 5 Mar 2026, gives 6,892 detained "between 2012 and 2025" [7]. **Judged authoritative:** the ministerial reply (official count, period stated). The cards' "since 2012" matches it; the attribution to Suaram should change.
- **22% versus 20–28%.** Malaysiakini reports both a 20–28% range and "about 22%" from "government statistics" [6] and does not reconcile them. The cards use the range, which is the safer choice.
- **Official outcome breakdown.** The ministerial reply [7] accounts for about 3,628 of 6,892 detainees (charged and sentenced, on trial, DNAA, acquitted). It does not say how the other ~3,264 ended, so it can neither confirm nor refute Suaram's range. The editor should not derive a release rate from it.

## SINCE PUBLICATION

- **25 May 2026:** SUHAKAM's Taiping Prison inquiry panel announced its findings: abuse and negligence, more than 100 inmates assaulted by about 60 wardens during a 17 Jan 2025 transfer, and one warden charged over the death of Gan Chin Eng [9]. The Rights card ("announced a public inquiry in 2025") is still accurate but now incomplete.
- **SOSMA amendments:** The Parliamentary Special Select Committee recommended in December 2025 that the 28-day period be reviewed [10]. No SOSMA amendment bill was found tabled by 8 Nov 2026. This was checked by search only, not against the Dewan Rakyat order papers.
