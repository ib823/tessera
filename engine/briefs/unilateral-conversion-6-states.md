## ISSUE

In *Indira Gandhi* (29 Jan 2018) the Federal Court held that Article 12(4), read with the Eleventh Schedule and the
Guardianship of Infants Act 1961, requires the consent of both parents before a certificate of conversion to Islam can
issue for a minor. In March 2023, M. Indira Gandhi and others filed an originating summons. It argued that provisions
in six state enactments and Federal Territories law still allow single-parent conversion of minors. The cards were
written while that challenge was pending. On 12 Jun 2026, after the source date, the Kuala Lumpur High Court dismissed it
for lack of standing without ruling on constitutionality.

## CONTRADICTIONS

- **Number of jurisdictions.** The headline says "six states and Federal Territories". Bernama (7) says "seven" places, which is
  six states plus the Federal Territories, consistent with the headline. Some outlets headlined "six states" alone. The
  named list in (7) and (8) is used.
- **Binding effect of Indira Gandhi.** Federal Court, May 2024 (5): binding throughout the nation. Federal Territories'
  position in the 2023 suit (6): not binding on the current FT provisions. Melaka High Court, May 2026 (9): binding only
  prospectively, which matches para 180 of the 2018 judgment (2). The Federal Court's own statements (2, 5) are
  authoritative over a litigant's submission.
- **Court level.** The view card says "Federal Court challenge". The filings and ruling were in the High Court (6, 7, 8).

## SINCE PUBLICATION

- **21 May 2026:** Melaka High Court held the 2018 ruling applies only to conversions registered after it (9).
- **12 Jun 2026:** Kuala Lumpur High Court (Datuk Aliza Sulaiman) dismissed the 2023 challenge for lack of locus standi,
  with no order as to costs. Constitutionality was not decided (7, 8). This overtakes the hook sub, the Rights sub, the
  Social sub and the view card.
