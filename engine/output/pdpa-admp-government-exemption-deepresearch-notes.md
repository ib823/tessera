# Deep-research report — integration notes (issue 2008)

**Source:** external deep-research report supplied by the operator (Google Doc,
"Malaysia's Algorithmic Exemptions / Regulatory Asymmetry in Malaysia's Data
Governance"), ~5,000 words, 50-source bibliography. Supplied during Phase 4 as
"Stage 2" but is a **research report, not a bias-audit JSON** — treated here as
an external research/fact-enrichment input (Stage 3-grade).

## Discipline applied
Per the Accuracy Standard, an AI-generated report is NOT a primary source. Every
fact below was independently re-verified against the primary instrument before
it was allowed to change a card.

## Material correction it surfaced (primary-verified, integrated)
- **"Malaysia passed no equivalent" was WRONG.** Malaysia has the **Cyber
  Security Act 2024 (Act 854)** (in force 26 Aug 2024; nacsa.gov.my/act854.php)
  and the **Data Sharing Act 2025 (Act 864)** (in force 28 Apr 2025;
  jdn.gov.my Act-864.pdf; s.18 = up to RM1m / 5y for officer misuse). fact-3
  rewritten to the accurate, sharper gap: those laws guard data security and
  punish leaking officers, but none grants a citizen an algorithmic-accountability
  right. Singapore dropped from the card (also exempts its public sector; uses
  internal AI governance, not statutory DPIAs — not a clean foil).

## Strengthening facts it surfaced (primary-verified, integrated)
- **PADU "Analytics-as-a-Service", May 2026, 26 government agencies**; use cases
  include scoring a borrower's **capability to repay a government loan** and
  ageing-population targeting (The Star / Malay Mail / FMT, 20 Apr 2026). Now
  in fact-2 — establishes PADU as an active automated-profiling engine.
- **PADU 30.7 million profiles as of April 2026** (SAYS / Malay Mail / FMT) —
  current figure adopted; distinct date from issue 1102's 30.4m (Jun 2025).
- **PDP Commissioner: Shariffah Rashidah Syed Othman**; ADMP grants a data-subject
  "Right to Refuse" solely-automated decisions and bars child-marketing profiling
  (added to brief, not needed in cards).
